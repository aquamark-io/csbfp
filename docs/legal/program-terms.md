## 1. What registration is, and what it isn't

Broker Registry is a private, voluntary registration program for individuals who broker or sell commercial financing in the United States. An active registration means only that the individual completed the required training, passed the assessment, and has a current paid registration term.

Registration is not a government license, regulatory registration, professional certification, accreditation, endorsement, background check, employment verification, or guarantee of competence, honesty, conduct, financial condition, or compliance with law. Broker Registry does not authorize anyone to offer, arrange, solicit, negotiate or broker financing. Registrants remain responsible for any licenses, registrations, disclosures and other legal requirements that apply to their work. A lookup result is not a substitute for independently checking someone's licensing, qualifications, identity, employer or regulatory standing. Recognition of the program by industry organizations does not mean they endorse or vouch for any individual registrant.

## 2. Eligibility and your account

To register, you must be at least 18 years old, broker or sell commercial financing in the United States, register only for yourself, provide accurate information, and be able to enter into these Terms. You may have only one account and one registration. Registration belongs to you, not to your employer, and cannot be transferred.

You must keep your information current, including your employment and contact details, and keep access to your email account secure, because sign-in codes are sent there. We may ask for reasonable additional information to resolve suspected duplicate accounts, fraud or eligibility questions, and we may decline a registration if the requirements are not met.

## 3. Training, assessment and activation

To activate a registration, you must complete the required training, score at least 80% on the assessment, and pay the annual registration fee. You may take the assessment up to 10 times; we may allow additional attempts on request. We may set reasonable rules to protect the integrity of the assessment.

The training is general educational information. It is not legal, tax or compliance advice, and the laws it describes can change.

Renewing each year requires payment only, not repeating the training or assessment, unless we announce new requirements in advance as described in Section 9.

## 4. Your registration number and the public lookup

When your registration is first activated, you receive one permanent registration number. It is never reassigned to anyone else and stays yours even if your registration lapses.

Anyone can check a registration by entering the exact registration number at broker-registry.org/verify. The lookup shows only your name, registration number, status, and the date your registration is valid through (or the date it expired). There is no directory and no search by name. Your date of birth, contact details, employer, ZIP code, assessment results and payment information are never shown publicly. We use rate limits and other measures to discourage automated queries and scraping, which are not allowed.

Status means:

- **Active:** training and assessment completed and the current annual term is paid.
- **Inactive:** a renewal payment failed and has not yet been paid.
- **Expired:** the paid term ended without renewal, for example after automatic renewal was canceled.

## 6. Fees and automatic renewal

**Price.** The annual registration fee is $99. We currently do not add sales tax. If tax is ever required, it will be shown before you pay.

**Your authorization.** At checkout you give a separate consent authorizing Broker Registry, through our payment processor Stripe, to charge $99 when you register and $99 every 12 months after that, on the anniversary of your activation date, until you cancel. The checkout page shows the amount, the renewal date and how to cancel.

**Reminder.** We email you 30 days before each renewal with the renewal date, the amount and how to cancel. If you do nothing, your registration renews and your saved payment method is charged.

**How to cancel.** You can cancel automatic renewal at any time online, from the Billing page in your account, without calling or contacting anyone. You can also email support@broker-registry.org for help. We email you a confirmation. Canceling stops future renewal charges; your registration stays Active through the end of the term you have paid for and then becomes Expired. You can turn automatic renewal back on before then.

**No refunds.** Fees are nonrefundable once charged and are not prorated, except where the law requires a refund. Nothing in these Terms limits cancellation or refund rights that cannot be waived by law.

**Failed payments.** If a renewal payment fails, we email you and your registration becomes Inactive. Pay the renewal through your account to reactivate it; your registration then runs to your normal renewal date, so paying late does not move your renewal date. If the renewal is never paid, or your registration has expired, you can rejoin at any time by paying again; you keep the same registration number and a new 12-month term starts on the date you pay.

**Price or billing changes.** We will give advance notice of any change to the renewal price or billing terms, and obtain your consent where the law requires it. You can always cancel before a change takes effect.
