Version 1.01

## 1. Overview

The Certified Small Business Finance Professional (CSBFP) program respects the privacy of applicants, certificants, and website visitors.

The program is owned by the Small Business Finance Association (SBFA) and administered by Duncan Consulting LLC.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to individuals.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Program, we may collect:

- **Personal information:** Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details.
- **Professional information:** Employer, company type, job function, employment history where requested, and related application details.
- **Certification information:** Training progress, assessment results, certification number, status, issuance date, and expiration date.
- **Payment information:** Transaction records, payment status, billing details, and limited payment-method information supplied by our payment processor.
- **Background-screening information:** Screening status, eligibility determinations, and relevant information provided by the authorized screening provider.
- **Technical information:** IP address, browser information, device details, login activity, and website usage information.

Additional information may be collected when necessary to administer the Program, investigate reported misconduct, or comply with legal obligations.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

1. Create and manage certification accounts.
2. Process applications and verify eligibility.
3. Deliver training and administer assessments.
4. Coordinate background screening.
5. Collect fees and manage annual renewals.
6. Issue and verify certification credentials.
7. Communicate about applications, renewals, and Program changes.
8. Investigate potential violations of professional standards.
9. Maintain website security and prevent fraud.
10. Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use background-screening information for unrelated marketing purposes.

## 4. Background Screening

Background screening is conducted through Checkr or another authorized provider.

Applicants may be directed to the screening provider to submit required information, receive disclosures, and provide authorization.

The Program receives screening information necessary to evaluate certification eligibility.

Screening information is treated as confidential and accessed only by authorized personnel with a legitimate administrative need.

Applicants may have additional rights to obtain, review, or dispute screening information directly through the screening provider or under applicable law.

## 5. Payments

Certification payments are processed through Stripe or another authorized payment provider.

Payment providers process card information under their own privacy and security practices.

The Program receives information necessary to confirm transactions, manage subscriptions, issue receipts, and address billing questions.

We do not intentionally store complete payment-card numbers in our application systems.

## 6. Public Certification Verification

CSBFP provides a public credential-verification service.

When a valid certification number is entered, the verification service may display:

- Certificant name
- Certification status
- Certification expiration date

Certification numbers are unique and permanently associated with the individual.

The verification service does not provide a searchable directory of individuals.

Background-screening results, application records, payment details, and reasons for suspension or revocation are not displayed publicly.

## 7. Information Sharing

We may share information with:

- **SBFA:** As program owner, for authorized oversight, certification governance, and administration.
- **Duncan Consulting LLC:** As program administrator, to operate and support CSBFP.
- **Service providers:** Including payment processors, background-screening providers, hosting services, database providers, and email-delivery services.
- **Legal authorities:** When required by law, valid legal process, or an applicable legal obligation.
- **Professional advisers:** When reasonably necessary for legal, compliance, security, or administrative purposes.

We do not sell applicant or certificant personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

We do not share background-screening information with employers or other third parties for independent employment decisions unless authorized or required by law.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Access to sensitive application and screening information is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to administer certification, maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, enforce Program requirements, and satisfy legal obligations.

Certification numbers and core certification records may be retained after expiration or revocation to preserve historical credential integrity.

Background-screening records and supporting information are retained only as necessary for legitimate Program purposes and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise securely disposed of in accordance with applicable retention practices.

## 10. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of eligible personal information.
- Obtain information about certain uses or disclosures.
- Exercise applicable privacy rights without unlawful discrimination.

Certain records may need to be retained for certification integrity, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or security purposes.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. We may verify identity before processing a request.

## 11. Email Communications

We send operational emails relating to applications, training, certification status, payments, renewals, security, and material Program updates.

These communications are necessary for Program administration.

Any optional promotional communications will be managed in accordance with applicable law and available unsubscribe preferences.

## 12. Cookies and Website Technology

The website may use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, session management, security, and essential functionality.

Additional analytics or tracking technologies, if used, will be disclosed and managed in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

## 13. Children's Privacy

CSBFP is intended for individuals 18 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly accept certification applications from individuals under 18.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in technology, Program operations, legal requirements, or information practices.

Material changes will be communicated through the website or other appropriate channels.

The updated policy will display its effective date.
